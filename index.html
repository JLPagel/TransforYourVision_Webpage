Healing Through Laying on of Hands

A Sacred Practice Rooted in Biblical Tradition

"They will place their hands on sick people, and they will get well."

— Mark 16:18

For many Christians, the question arises: "Can Reiki align with my faith?" The answer is a resounding yes. Reiki is not a religion—it's a healing modality that channels the same universal life force that God breathed into all creation. It is a practice deeply aligned with the biblical tradition of healing through the laying on of hands.

🙏

Jesus Healed Through Touch

Throughout the Gospels, Jesus laid hands on the sick to heal them. He touched lepers, the blind, and the paralyzed, channeling divine healing energy through His presence and touch. Reiki follows this same sacred tradition—using gentle touch or the placement of hands to facilitate God's healing power.

"He touched her hand and the fever left her" (Matthew 8:15)

✨

We Are Called to Heal

Jesus didn't keep healing to Himself—He commissioned His disciples to do the same. "Heal the sick, raise the dead, cleanse those who have leprosy" (Matthew 10:8). As believers, we are called to be vessels of God's healing love. Reiki is simply one way to answer that calling, using our hands as instruments of divine grace.

"The prayer offered in faith will make the sick person well" (James 5:15)

🕊️

God's Energy Flows Through All Creation

The Bible speaks of the Holy Spirit as the breath of God, the life force that animates all living things. "The Spirit of God has made me; the breath of the Almighty gives me life" (Job 33:4). Reiki works with this same divine energy—the life force that God placed within us. When we channel Reiki, we're simply opening ourselves to be conduits of the healing love that God has always intended for His children.

"In Him we live and move and have our being" (Acts 17:28)

💛

Love Is the Greatest Healer

At its core, Reiki is about channeling love—pure, unconditional, divine love. The Bible tells us that "God is love" (1 John 4:8), and that love never fails. When I practice Reiki, I am praying for you, holding space for God's healing presence to work through me. It's not about my power—it's about surrendering to God's will and allowing His love to flow where it's needed most.

"And over all these virtues put on love, which binds them all together" (Colossians 3:14)

A Practice That Honors Your Faith

You don't have to set aside your Christian beliefs to receive Reiki—in fact, many find that Reiki deepens their faith. It creates space for prayer, meditation, and connection with God. During our sessions, you're welcome to pray, invite the Holy Spirit, or simply rest in the knowledge that you are held by divine love.

Reiki doesn't replace prayer or medical care—it complements them. It's one more way to invite God's healing presence into your life, to care for the body God gave you, and to honor the truth that we are fearfully and wonderfully made.

If you've been seeking healing—physically, emotionally, or spiritually—know that God meets us where we are. Reiki is simply an invitation to open your heart and hands to receive what He has already freely given: abundant life, peace, and wholeness.

What to Expect in Your Session

✨ Reiki Master • 10+ Years Experience

With a Master's degree in Reiki and a decade of practice, I bring deep expertise and intuitive guidance to every session. Whether we meet in person or connect virtually, the healing energy flows with the same powerful intention.

Before Your Session

We'll have a brief conversation about your intentions, any physical or emotional concerns, and what you hope to experience. This helps me tailor the session to support your unique journey.

During Your Session

In-Person: You'll lie fully clothed on a comfortable massage table while I place my hands gently on or above specific areas of your body, allowing universal life force energy to flow where it's needed most.

Virtual: From the comfort of your own sacred space, you'll relax while I channel healing energy to you remotely. Many clients report the same profound experiences as in-person sessions—warmth, tingling, deep peace, and emotional release.

There's no "right" way to experience Reiki—your body knows exactly what it needs.

After Your Session

We'll take time to discuss your experience, and I'll share any intuitive insights that came through. You may feel energized, deeply relaxed, or emotionally lighter. I'll provide guidance on integrating your healing and may suggest practices to support your continued well-being.

Virtual Sessions: Reiki energy transcends time and space. Virtual sessions are just as powerful as in-person work—you'll simply need a quiet space where you can relax undisturbed for the duration of our session. We'll connect via video call before and after the energy work.
