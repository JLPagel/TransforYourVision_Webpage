# Transform Your Vision — Main Page/Practice

This site is part of Jennifer Pagel’s portfolio and service ecosystem, focused on helping small businesses and startups bring their ideas to life through smart, human-centered digital solutions. It’s designed to inspire action, build trust, and showcase the value of investing in thoughtful web development.

## 🌟 Purpose

Transform Your Vision is built to:
- Invite entrepreneurs to see their digital presence as a strategic asset
- Present coaching and development services in a clear, compelling way
- Guide visitors through a trust-building journey from story to solution
- Offer transparent pricing and a seamless contact experience

## 🛠 Tech Stack

- **HTML, CSS, JavaScript** — for responsive layout and styling
- **GitHub Pages** — for hosting and deployment
- **Google Tag Manager & GA4** — for analytics and conversion tracking
- **Trustpilot Integration** — for credibility and social proof
- **Zoho CRM (via Zoho Flow)** — for lead capture and onboarding automation

## 📌 Features

- Strategic layout: About, Investment Opportunity, Pricing, Contact
- Embedded GitHub and YouTube links for portfolio and tutorials
- Trustpilot badge and review widget
- Custom contact form with backend automation
- Mobile-first responsive design
- Playful UI elements (emojis, color accents) for warmth and engagement
